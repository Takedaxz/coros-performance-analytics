## Easy (E) and Marathon-pace (M) volume

Use the Daniels vocabulary to establish the physiological objective of every run:
- **E (Easy Running)**: Primary purpose is accumulating aerobic volume, promoting capillary growth, conditioning connective tissues, and reinforcing relaxed mechanics with minimal autonomic fatigue. Finish every easy run feeling capable of running again tomorrow; never treat an easy day as a tempo or personal best.
- **M (Marathon-Type Effort)**: Prolonged race-specific steady work. Develops marathon pace rhythm, metabolic efficiency, fueling tolerance, and muscular stamina against late-run degradation. Most potent when embedded inside progressive long runs.

Source: Jack Daniels, *Daniels' Running Formula*, 4th ed. Human Kinetics, 2021.

## Controlled Threshold (T) and steady lactate state

Threshold training elevates sustainable aerobic pace by raising the second lactate threshold (LT2 / critical speed):
- **Structure**: Continuous tempos (20–30 minutes) or cruise intervals (e.g. 3 x 8 minutes, 4 x 2 km with short ~60–90 second recoveries).
- **Execution**: Threshold work must be controlled and sustainable, not an all-out time trial. If pace rapidly decays or form breaks down, the effort has drifted into severe intensity, altering the metabolic stimulus and dramatically increasing recovery cost.

Source: Jack Daniels, *Daniels' Running Formula*, 4th ed.; [Bourdon et al., training-load monitoring consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28463642/).

## Interval (I) and Repetition (R) speed mechanics

- **I (Interval / VO2max Work)**: Expands the aerobic ceiling and accumulates time near maximal oxygen uptake (e.g. 5 x 1 km, 6 x 800m at ~3K–5K effort). Work-to-rest intervals (typically 1:1 or slightly less) must preserve target pace. If recovery is cut too short, mechanics collapse and the session degenerates into non-functional lactate distress.
- **R (Repetition / Speed & Economy)**: Targets neuromuscular coordination, foot strike stiffness, biomechanical efficiency, and muscle recruitment (e.g. 200m–400m reps). Requires full recovery between repetitions so each effort is executed fast, relaxed, and mechanically precise.

Source: Jack Daniels, *Daniels' Running Formula*, 4th ed. Human Kinetics, 2021.
