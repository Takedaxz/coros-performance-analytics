## Official HYROX Station Distances, Weights, and Reps

HYROX alternates eight 1 km runs with eight functional workout stations (8 km total running). Below are the exact official weights, distances, and reps for every division:

| Workout Station | Women Open / Women Doubles / Women Relay / (Women of) Mixed Relay | Men Open / Men Doubles / Mixed Doubles / Men Relay / (Men of) Mixed Relay / Women Pro / Women Doubles Pro | Men Pro / Men Doubles Pro |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SkiErg** | 1000m | 1000m | 1000m |
| **Sled Push** (50m) | 102kg *(incl. sled)* | 152kg *(incl. sled)* | 202kg *(incl. sled)* |
| **Sled Pull** (50m) | 78kg *(incl. sled)* | 103kg *(incl. sled)* | 153kg *(incl. sled)* |
| **Burpee Broad Jump** | 80m | 80m | 80m |
| **Rowing** | 1000m | 1000m | 1000m |
| **Farmers Carry** (200m) | 2x16kg | 2x24kg | 2x32kg |
| **Sandbag Lunges** (100m) | 10kg | 20kg | 30kg |
| **Wall Balls** | 100 reps (4kg) | 100 reps (6kg) | 100 reps (9kg) |

Source: Official HYROX Division Competition Standards.
