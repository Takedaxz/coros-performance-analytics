## HYROX race demands

HYROX alternates eight 1 km runs with eight functional workout stations. Training should therefore develop sustainable running under fatigue, station-specific movement skill, and transitions without repeatedly turning every session into a maximal simulation. Use the official current rulebook for station standards, distances, loads, and penalties because they can change by season and division.

Source: [HYROX race format](https://hyrox.com/the-fitness-race/) and [HYROX Singles Rulebook 2025/26](https://maintain.hyrox.com/rulebooks/HYROX_RulebookSingles_EN.pdf).

## Mixed-modal conditioning

For HYROX or CrossFit-style training, separate skill/strength development from high-fatigue conditioning often enough to preserve technique. Progress one variable at a time: running volume, station load, density, or complexity. When performance degrades because of pain, dizziness, or unusually poor recovery, stop escalating and reassess rather than adding more intensity.

Source: [IOC consensus on load and injury risk](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27535989/).

## Strength alongside endurance

Strength sessions should support the current endurance goal, not compete with every key run. Preserve high-quality compound movement practice, allow recovery before key running sessions, and reduce nonessential lifting volume when endurance load peaks. This is general programming guidance, not individualized rehabilitation or a substitute for coaching movement assessment.

Source: [ISSN position stand: protein and exercise](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28642676/).
